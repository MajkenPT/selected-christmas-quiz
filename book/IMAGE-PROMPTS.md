# Illustrations for ELF & ORDER

One picture per episode, saved in this folder as `ep01.jpg` … `ep13.jpg` (`.png`, `.webp` and `.jpeg` also work).
Until a file exists, the book shows a placeholder with two Santa hats.

**Format:** square (1:1), about 1200 × 1200 px, under ~400 KB each (run them through squoosh.app or similar).
The episode title is printed under the picture in the book, so generate **without** headline text in the image.
Signs and mugs that are part of the scene keep their words (see each prompt).

> The files in this folder are public URLs. Chapters are locked on the server, but a picture can be opened by anyone who
> guesses `/book/img/ep09.jpg`. If surprises matter, give the files non-guessable names (e.g. `ep09-7fk2.jpg`) and
> tell me, so the book points to them.

## How to get the faces right

Use an image tool that accepts reference photos (Gemini, ChatGPT, Midjourney with `--cref`, etc.). Attach the photo of
the two colleagues to **every** prompt and start with the character line below. Make episode 01 first, check that both are
recognisable, then reuse that picture as an extra reference for the rest so the faces stay consistent.
Make sure both colleagues are fine with being turned into elves before this goes out.

**Character line (paste at the start of every prompt):**

> Use the attached photo as the face and hair reference. Two young women, the two elves of the story.
> JOHANNE: *[describe: hair, eyes]*. LINE: *[describe: hair, eyes]*.
> Both wear red Santa/elf hats with white fur trim and red-and-green Nordic knit Christmas jumpers. Keep their faces, hair and
> proportions true to the photo.

**Style line (paste at the end of every prompt):**

> Warm, glossy, slightly cartoonish editorial photo style, shallow depth of field, cosy golden fairy lights, a modern
> Scandinavian fashion-company office as the background, square 1:1, no headline text, no watermark.
